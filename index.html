Admissions Criteria

  • Completion of secondary education minimum qualification 8th pass (for Diploma courses)
  • Minimum Primary School Qualified (for Certificate courses)
  • Passing score in the entrance examination (if applicable)

Steps to Apply:

  1. Visit our Nearest Branch of ApnaInstitute.
  2. Submit necessary documents, such as academic transcripts and identification proof.
  3. Pay the application donation securely through our online payment gateway.
  4. Receive confirmation of admission and details regarding orientation.

About Us

Apna Institute is dedicated to providing premium higher education in computer science and related fields at an affordable price. Our vision is to make advanced learning accessible to everyone, fostering a community of innovation, excellence, and lifelong learning.

Our programs are designed to equip students with cutting-edge skills and knowledge, ensuring they are prepared to meet the demands of the modern technological landscape. Our experienced faculty members are committed to mentoring and guiding each student towards achieving their academic and professional goals.
